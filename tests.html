Scroll depth

		

			Content mode:
			article (default) ·
			data-cabin-content ·
			whole document
		

		

			Layout:
			window scroll (default) ·
			app shell (inner pane) ·
			app shell + data-cabin-scroll-root ·
			growing feed ·
			short main, tall footer ·
			scrolling sidebar (must be ignored)
		

		

			What to expect. app shell and pinned: depth tracks
			the pane, not -1. feed: reading two screens of an
			eventual ten reports about 20%, never 100%. short: a glance
			reports a fraction of the footer-trimmed document (mode 2), not a
			trusted 100%. sidebar: scrolling the sidebar alone leaves depth
			where the window put it.
